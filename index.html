01 / ABOUT

From components
to dependable
systems.

I am interested in what makes a system work reliably when conditions change.

My background in robotics connects sensing, computation, communication and physical execution. Through autonomous robotics and networking projects, I have explored the practical trade-offs between performance, correctness and limited computing resources.

My interests span robust robotic perception, efficient edge inference and dependable software infrastructure. I enjoy translating these questions into working implementations—and understanding what happens when theory meets the real world.
